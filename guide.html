Run sales, track commissions, and keep every payment in order.

Neatwork is a business operations workspace for agencies and teams that manage customers, sales, invoices, payments, and commission workflows.

One workspace for customer records, sales, payments, and agency operations

Who can use Neatwork

Cleaning agenciesSales agenciesRecruitment and referrals Real estate and property agentsAffiliate and reseller networks Marketing agenciesDelivery and freelance teamsCustom agency services

Use the workspace

  1. Add customers. Save a customer or company name and their contact details.
  2. Create a quote. Add products or services, quantities, prices, tax, and notes. Save common line items in Settings to reuse them.
  3. Send for approval. Email or text a quote, or share its private response link. Customers can accept or decline sent quotes.
  4. Create an invoice. Turn an accepted quote into an invoice and set its due date.
  5. Track payment. Send an online payment link or record an offline payment. Partial payments, payment history, refund records, and installment schedules are available.
  6. Keep records. Review open quotes, overdue invoices, and payments on the overview. Archive customers or quotes when you need to retain them outside active lists.

Commission and agency management

Neatwork is intended to grow into a commission and agency operations platform. The following capabilities are planned and are not currently available in the workspace:

Planned

Commission tracking

Fixed, percentage, tiered, and referral commission rules with pending, approved, and paid payout states.

Planned

Teams and targets

Staff and agent roles, teams or branches, individual targets, and performance reporting.

Planned

Flexible agency workflows

Configurable work types for sales, property transactions, recruitment placements, referrals, deliveries, projects, and custom services.

Planned

Relationship history

Customer activity, sales and service history, notes, follow-ups, and referral sources.

Planned

Operations audit trail

Consistent activity history across customer, transaction, quote, invoice, payment, and commission records.

Commission calculations, staff roles, targets, configurable work types, and the broader agency-management features listed as planned are not part of the current application. Current payment records do not calculate or pay commissions.
